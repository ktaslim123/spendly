Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic usage information, such as login timestamps, to help keep your account secure.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the expense tracking features of Spendly — such as generating spending summaries, category breakdowns, and historical reports. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting or email delivery services) solely to operate the service, and only to the extent necessary.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account dashboard.
